<b>NEXUS MATCHLINK v0.4</b> — auto-accept your Dota 2 / CS2 match from your phone.

Never miss a game again. The app watches your screen, and the moment the
Accept button appears it pushes a notification to your Telegram with an
"ACCEPT MATCH" button. Tap it on your phone — the app restores the game
window and clicks Accept for you.

<b>What it does:</b>
• Instant Telegram push when a match is found
• Accept with one tap from your phone
• Works in the background — you can minimize the game
• Supports Dota 2 and CS2

<b>How to install:</b>
1. Download the ZIP from the pinned post
2. Unzip anywhere
3. Run NexusMatchlink.exe
4. Press CONNECT TG, send the 6-digit code to the bot, and queue up

<b>About safety & SmartScreen:</b>
If Windows shows a SmartScreen warning — that's normal: the exe is not
signed with a digital certificate, like most small indie utilities. Click
"More info" → "Run anyway". The app runs locally and only talks to your
own Telegram.
